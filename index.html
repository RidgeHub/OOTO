✝ Welcome to OOTO.net ✝

Hey and GOD BLESS you for stopping by the official home of Out of the Ordinary on the World Wide Web! We are so pumped that you found us and we hope this site blesses your socks off.

"...chosen out of the world..." — John 15:19

OOTO started back in '97 in a basement at Northside Baptist Church in Indianapolis with nothing but a second-hand drum kit, two guitars, and a burning desire to praise the Lord through rock and roll. We believe God can use ANY sound to bring glory to His name — even distortion pedals!

Our mission is simple: bring the lost to the cross and fire up the saved. If you've been touched by our music, please sign the guestbook below and let us know. We read every single entry!
