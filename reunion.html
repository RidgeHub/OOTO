Our Story

From a basement
to the altar

It started in 1997 in a basement at Northside Baptist Church in Indianapolis — four guys with second-hand gear, unshakeable faith, and an unquenchable need to make noise for the glory of God.

Out of the Ordinary wasn't just a band. OOTO was a movement that swept through youth groups, church camps, and Christian music festivals across the Midwest and beyond. At a time when CCM was finding its rock voice, OOTO was one of the bands leading the charge in the pews.

"God can use any sound to bring glory to His name — even distortion pedals."

After years of touring, hundreds of shows, and countless lives touched at the altar, Out of the Ordinary quietly stepped back in 2004. Now, 25 years after that first rehearsal, the four original members are coming home.

1997

Self-titled debut recorded over two weekends. Sold out of 500 CDs at Acquire the Fire, Columbus.

Breakthrough sophomore album. Midwest tour hits 40+ cities. Creation Fest appearance.

Peak Years

National touring. Radio play on WAY-FM. Third album "The Overcomer" released.

2004

Final Show

Last performance at Northside Baptist — where it all began. A full circle moment.
